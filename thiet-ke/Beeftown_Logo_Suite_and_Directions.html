Logo suite

One brand, four logo types — each for a different job. Built on the Modern Meat Market palette. Use the wordmark as the default; the monogram and emblem are for small or stamp-like spaces.

01

The primary logo. Default for signage, headers and packaging. Two-tone (BEEF + TOWN) for impact; goes solid on busy or small contexts.
